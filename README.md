# Practical Governance Guide for OpenClaw

A practical governance guide for thinking clearly about roles, decisions, accountability, conflict, and process in OpenClaw-related work.

This guide is meant to support better community and project coordination. It is not legal advice, official policy, or a binding constitution unless OpenClaw or a specific project explicitly adopts it.

## What this is

This repo is a practical governance resource.

It can help with:

- clarifying roles and responsibilities
- distinguishing proposals from decisions
- making decision-making more legible
- recording decisions and review points
- thinking through accountability
- creating lightweight process before conflict escalates
- identifying where authority, consultation, and implementation differ

## What this is not

This repo is not:

- legal advice
- official OpenClaw policy unless adopted
- a universal governance model
- a replacement for maintainers, stewards, or community decision-making
- a way to avoid hard conversations
- a guarantee that governance problems will be solved

See [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md) for more detail.

## Who this is for

This guide may be useful for:

- project maintainers
- contributors
- community stewards
- working group leads
- people drafting governance proposals
- people trying to make decisions easier to understand and revisit

## Core idea

Governance is not just values language. Governance is how a project decides, records, acts, reviews, and repairs.

A useful governance process should help people answer:

- Who can decide?
- Who should be consulted?
- Who is affected?
- What counts as approval?
- Where is the decision recorded?
- Who implements the decision?
- When is the decision reviewed?
- What happens if the decision causes harm or stops working?
