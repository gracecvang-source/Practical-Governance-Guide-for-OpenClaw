# Scope and Limits

This guide is a practical governance resource for OpenClaw-related planning, discussion, and community structure.

It is meant to help people think clearly about roles, decisions, accountability, conflict, and process.

## What this guide is

This guide is:

- a practical governance aid
- a starting point for discussion
- a collection of reusable structures and prompts
- a way to make decision-making more legible
- a tool for clarifying roles, responsibilities, and process

## What this guide is not

This guide is not:

- legal advice
- official policy unless explicitly adopted
- a binding constitution by itself
- a substitute for maintainers, stewards, or community decision-making
- a universal model for every project
- a guarantee that governance problems will be solved

## How to use it

Use this guide to ask better questions before decisions become messy.

For example:

- Who has authority to decide this?
- Who should be consulted?
- Who is affected by the decision?
- What is the process for disagreement?
- What happens after a decision is made?
- How will the decision be recorded?
- When should the decision be reviewed?

## Adoption note

A governance guide becomes binding only when a project, team, or community explicitly adopts it.

Until then, treat it as a reference, not authority.

## Legal note

This guide does not provide legal advice. If a decision has legal, financial, employment, nonprofit, contractual, or regulatory consequences, consult qualified professionals.

## Privacy note

Do not use public governance documents to expose private conflicts, personal details, or sensitive internal information.

When possible, turn private incidents into generalized process lessons without naming people or sharing unnecessary details.
